FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What do I get with the Free plan?

The Free plan gives you access to workout previews, our community forum, the weekly newsletter, and a personal profile. It's a great way to explore what ComboMaster offers before committing to a paid plan.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes will be reflected in your next billing cycle. You can also start Free and upgrade whenever you're ready.

Is there a free trial on paid plans?

Yes! All paid plans come with a 7-day free trial. Cancel anytime before the trial ends and you won't be charged. You'll revert to the Free plan automatically.

How do I cancel my membership?

You can cancel anytime from your dashboard. Your access will continue until the end of your current billing period, then you'll be moved to the Free plan.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and Apple Pay. All payments are processed securely. The Free plan requires no payment method at all.
