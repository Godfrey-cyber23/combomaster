FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does membership cost?

Our plans range from K350/month (Starter) to K600/month (Elite), with the Premium plan at K475/month. Quarterly billing saves you 10% and yearly billing saves you 20% — use the toggle above to see the exact figures.

Can I switch plans or billing periods later?

Yes! You can upgrade, downgrade, or change between monthly, quarterly, and yearly billing at any time. Changes take effect in your next billing cycle.

Is there a free trial on paid plans?

Yes! All plans come with a 7-day free trial. Cancel anytime before the trial ends and you won't be charged.

How do I cancel my membership?

You can cancel anytime — there are no lock-in contracts. Your access continues until the end of your current billing period.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, and all major debit and credit cards. All prices are in Zambian Kwacha (K).

What are your opening hours?

Mon–Fri: 05:00–21:00 · Sat: 08:00–17:00 · Sun: 08:00–13:00. We are open every single day — including public holidays.
