FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes will be reflected in your next billing cycle.

Is there a free trial?

We offer a 7-day free trial on all plans. Cancel anytime before the trial ends and you won't be charged.

How do I cancel my membership?

You can cancel anytime from your dashboard. Your access will continue until the end of your current billing period.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and Apple Pay. All payments are processed securely.
